## 🤖 2. Sub-Agent Responsibilities & Model Allocation

1. **Main Orchestrator (Task Dispatcher & Dossier Merger):**
   * Manages project state, coordinates asynchronous sub-agent executions, and merges research data.
2. **Sub-Agent 1 (Research & Fact Agent):**
   * Analyzes specific micro-market buyer friction points, infrastructure growth (Ring Road, Metro, Airport), and RERA Rajasthan compliance.
3. **Sub-Agent 2 (SEO & Keyword Strategist):**
   * Builds high-intent keyword clusters, click-worthy Meta Titles (<60 chars), Meta Descriptions (<155 chars), and Schema FAQ targets.
4. **Sub-Agent 3 (Master Writer Agent):**
   * Emulates Nine Degree’s authoritative advisory voice across 3 editorial archetypes (Narrative & Buyer Psychology, Strategic Advisory & Market Positioning, Growth & Performance Marketing), integrating the human's **Expert Point Box**.
5. **Sub-Agent 4 (Architectural Visual Director):**
   * Renders 2 photorealistic luxury architectural images via the open-source **FLUX.1** engine without stock-photo repetition.
6. **Sub-Agent 5 (Quality & SEO Auditor Agent):**
   * Performs automated auditing, checks for 6 core AI cliché tells (`delve into`, `tapestry`, `testament`, `crucial role`, `in today's fast-paced world`, `navigating the landscape`), and computes an audited quality score.

## 🌟 3. Key Human-in-the-Loop Features

* **The "Expert Point Box":** Allows human strategists to inject confidential client data, builder quotes, or unreleased project metrics that the AI treats as empirical facts.
* **Custom Visual & Photo Guidance:** User can dictate the architectural theme, materials (Rajasthan sandstone, Italian marble), and lighting.
* **Real-Time Audit Bar:** Displays the audited quality score, exact word count, AI cliché scan results, and target SEO keywords right above the article.
