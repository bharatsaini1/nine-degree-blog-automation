## Introduction: The Macro Landscape
In the Indian residential market, marketing budgets are routinely burned by applying uniform creative strategies across radically disparate buyer psychographics.

## The Core Divergence: Exclusivity vs Financial Velocity

Luxury properties cannot be sold via discount triggers or generic lead-gen forms. They require bespoke architectural narratives and curated site-visit experiences.

Mid-segment campaigns thrive on Meta and Google Search with high-frequency retargeting. Luxury buyers demand private banking tie-ups, invitation-only previews, and experiential marketing.
